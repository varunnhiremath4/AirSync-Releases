# AirSync

**No Touch. All Flex.**

Control your Windows PC with hand gestures and a webcam. Point to move the cursor, pinch to click, scroll with your hand, or bring up Window Orbit to switch apps.

## Download for Windows

[Download the latest AirSync installer](https://github.com/varunnhiremath4/AirSync-Releases/releases/latest/download/AirSync-Setup.exe)

AirSync runs on Windows 11 PCs with a 64-bit Intel or AMD processor. You’ll need a built-in webcam, USB webcam, or compatible phone connected as a Windows camera.

## Get started

1. Download and run `AirSync-Setup.exe`.
2. Open **AirSync** from the Windows Start menu.
3. In **Settings**, select your camera. Start hand tracking and check that your whole hand is visible.
4. Point to move the cursor. Briefly pinch your thumb and index finger to click.

Press **Ctrl + Shift + Space** to pause gesture control.

## The nine gestures

| Control | Gesture |
| --- | --- |
| Move the cursor | Point and move your index finger. |
| Left-click | Briefly pinch thumb and index finger. |
| Drag and drop | Hold the index pinch, move your hand, then release. |
| Right-click | Pinch thumb and middle finger. |
| Quick app switch | Pinch thumb and ring finger. |
| Window Orbit | Pinch thumb and pinky to open the app selector. |
| Air Scroll | Turn four fingers sideways and move your hand up, down, left, or right. |
| Pause or resume | Hold a closed fist for three seconds. |
| Air Zoom | Move both open hands apart to zoom in or closer to zoom out. |
